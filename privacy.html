3. Why We Process It

  • To administer entries and run the prize draw, as required to fulfil the competition rules you agreed to;
  • To contact winners and arrange prize collection;
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations under the Consumer Protection Act and its Regulations, which require competition records to be kept for a minimum of 3 years; and
  • To produce anonymised or aggregated reporting on entries (e.g. entries per suburb), which does not identify you individually.

4. Who We Share It With

Your information is stored in Google Sheets and processed via Google Apps Script, acting as our data processor. If you share your location, the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service solely to look up your suburb name — no name, surname, or mobile number is sent as part of that lookup. We do not sell your information or share it with any other third party.

5. How Long We Keep It

We retain competition entry records for a minimum of 3 years after the competition ends, in line with the record-keeping requirements of the Consumer Protection Act Regulations. After this period, records are securely deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of what personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information;
  • Object to further processing of your information, subject to our record-keeping obligations above; and
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za if you believe your rights have been infringed.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

7. Security

Staff access to entry data (for prize redemption and winner selection) is restricted behind PIN-protected tools. Raw entry data is not publicly accessible.
